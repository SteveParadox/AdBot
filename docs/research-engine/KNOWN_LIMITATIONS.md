# Known limitations

- A real search provider and model are required to validate live research quality. The fixture test exercises the complete worker flow with simulated responses. The Chromium fallback runs against controlled source material.
- Ranking combines weighted lexical relevance, phrase proximity, reciprocal ranks and registrable-domain diversity. An opt-in, bounded existing-model reranker can assess semantic relevance of search snippets. Source signals are heuristics, not verified reputation or factual accuracy. Text-similarity grouping can miss paraphrased syndication and shared ownership, or conservatively group unrelated sources with extensive common text. Different source groups do not prove editorial independence. No vector index, trained cross-encoder, ownership database or independent reputation service is shipped.
- Semantic verification is a second constrained model call using the same configured provider chain. Exact citations, quoted evidence and independent domains are checked deterministically where applicable. Model agreement still needs judgment, and adversarial or subtle contradictions can survive the verifier.
- Automatic classification uses rules. Specialized queries can be overridden manually. Deep research uses bounded subquestions and model-assessed evidence coverage to drive targeted follow-up searches. The coverage sample is bounded; subquestions and semantic judgments can be incomplete, and the final answer check establishes evidence reuse rather than independently proving full answer coverage. Plans persist at task completion, not after each round. It does not maintain a general-purpose knowledge graph or independently prove the complete space of potentially relevant sources.
- Sources may lack reliable publication dates. Provider freshness support differs. Public HTML, XHTML, plain text and bounded text-based PDF extraction are supported. PDF parsing examines only the first 40 physical pages within byte, text, table and execution limits. Ruled-table extraction is heuristic; scanned PDFs require unsupported OCR, complex layouts may be incomplete, and printed page labels may differ from physical page numbers. PDF metadata creation dates are not publication dates. Encrypted PDFs, authenticated sites, paywalls, CAPTCHA challenges, cross-origin browser scripts and interactive browser filtering remain outside the retrieval implementation. Office documents are not supported. Linux parser resource limits are not a full OS sandbox; other platforms have a wall-clock deadline but no equivalent hard memory/CPU limits.
- Shopping extracts structured Product and Offer markup, with a conservative scoped HTML fallback for explicit product and price observations. Many retailers omit price, inventory, warranty, shipping or variant details; CSS-dependent visibility, unmarked product layouts and ambiguous prices remain limitations. Exact identifier/configuration grouping does not establish equivalence for missing identifiers or unknown condition. Supported requirement and workload checks are deterministic heuristics, not broad semantic suitability or performance guarantees. Currency codes are normalized and offers are compared within a currency; foreign exchange conversion, historical price tracking, automatic product image loading and review-text analysis remain unavailable. Availability describes the retrieved retailer record at the recorded time.
- The app streams observable progress and publishes verified answer segments after the answer is committed. Raw model tokens are withheld during verification.
- SQL shares provider rate limits and cooldowns across workers. Search and document caches are worker-local. Recovery can repeat a read-only request within the original deadline and the persisted remaining budgets. Partial source collections are committed at task completion.
- Process caches are tenant-keyed and expire independently of persisted conversation deletion. A restarted worker begins with empty caches.

- Expanded synthesis uses bounded, independently checked claim batches and deterministic report assembly. It does not guarantee exhaustive semantic coverage or reconcile every disagreement between different batches. Oversized structured records may remain outside model context; unprocessed evidence and model-reported limitations mark the result partial. Actual report length depends on configured output, request, time, call and token limits.

- Conversation memory selects from at most 32 completed turns within one owner-scoped conversation. Reference resolution and supported constraint changes are deterministic, not general semantic coreference. Ambiguous references are disclosed; older references and unsupported constraints may be missed. Prior answers remain untrusted and are not accepted as current evidence. No cross-conversation memory or vector index is used.

- Checkpointed progress survives lease recovery within the original deadline and charged budgets. In-flight operations can be repeated if interrupted before a durable boundary; transient navigation candidates may be lost. Oversized or policy-incompatible checkpoints may not restore. Redis global capacity, real multi-worker failover, and performance are not yet validated.

- Publication freshness is assessed per claim from explicit publisher-declared dates; missing, conflicting or ambiguous date metadata remains unknown. Fresh publication dates do not prove recent events. Numerical corroboration handles common units, currencies, signs and magnitudes but is not a semantic entailment engine, arbitrary unit converter or calculation verifier. Retailer observations are time-bounded, not guarantees of current checkout price or inventory.
